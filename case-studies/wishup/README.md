<h2>📖 Comprehensive Case Study</h2>

<details open>
  <summary><b>🔴 Situation & Core Challenge</b></summary>
  <br />
  <p>Wishup provides remote talent (VAs, EAs, PAs, bookkeepers) to U.S. SMBs and executives. Target customers search by problem (<i>"cost of virtual assistant", "how to delegate calendar management"</i>) rather than brand name. Organic performance faced four compounding constraints:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><b>Technical Debt:</b> Crawl budget waste from index bloat, canonicalization errors, and Core Web Vitals bottlenecks.</li>
    <li><b>Intent & On-Page Mismatch:</b> Search volume was concentrated on top-of-funnel informational articles lacking conversion UX, E-E-A-T signals, or clear calls-to-action.</li>
    <li><b>Content & Backlink Gaps:</b> Unorganized content hubs caused internal keyword cannibalization, while primary commercial pages lacked high-authority contextual backlinks.</li>
    <li><b>Algorithmic Instability:</b> Frequent Google Core Updates caused ranking shifts with no diagnostic workflow to isolate root causes from systemic volatility.</li>
  </ul>
</details>

<details open>
  <summary><b>🟡 Task & Strategic Ownership</b></summary>
  <br />
  <p>I owned U.S. organic acquisition end-to-end with five core mandates:</p>
  <ol>
    <li>Identify true root-cause bottlenecks instead of chasing surface symptoms.</li>
    <li>Prioritize technical, on-page, content, and backlink fixes by commercial ROI over technical severity alone.</li>
    <li>Align On-Page, Content SEO, and link acquisition to build compounding topical authority.</li>
    <li>Convert findings into developer-ready specifications and actionable content briefs.</li>
    <li>Stabilize organic lead volume through core algorithm volatility.</li>
  </ol>
</details>

<details open>
  <summary><b>🟢 Action & Operational Framework</b></summary>
  <br />
  <h4>1. Technical Architecture Optimization (20+ Audits)</h4>
  <p>Executed 20+ audits covering log files, indexation parameters, <code>robots.txt</code>, structured data, and rendering performance. Fixed index bloat and redirected link equity to primary revenue-generating URLs.</p>
  
  <h4>2. On-Page, Search Intent & Content SEO (100+ Pages)</h4>
  <p>Rebuilt the site content structure around buyer funnel stage and topic cluster architecture:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><b>TOFU (Top of Funnel):</b> Educational guides focused on broad awareness and discovery.</li>
    <li><b>MOFU (Middle of Funnel):</b> Comparison matrices ("In-house vs Remote EA") capturing evaluation traffic.</li>
    <li><b>BOFU (Bottom of Funnel):</b> High-intent service landing pages rebuilt with trust signals (E-E-A-T), transparent pricing, optimized title/heading hierarchies, and call-booking CTAs.</li>
  </ul>

  <h4>3. Strategic Backlink Acquisition</h4>
  <p>Focused off-page efforts on acquiring contextual, relevant B2B backlinks to pass link equity directly to high-converting MOFU and BOFU target pages rather than relying solely on home page authority.</p>

  <h4>4. Operational Systems & Analytics (15+ SOPs/Dashboards)</h4>
  <p>Built 15+ GA4 and Search Console dashboards tracking indexing health, CTR, bounce rates, and lead pipelines, governed by a standardized operational loop: <i>Evidence → Diagnosis → Priority → Owner → Deadline</i>.</p>

  <h4>5. Prioritization & Engineering Alignment</h4>
  <p>Applied an Impact / Confidence / Ease (ICE) scoring lens to ensure engineering sprint capacity was allocated strictly to high-ROI growth tasks.</p>
</details>

<h2>🛠️ Strategic Deep Dives</h2>

<details>
  <summary><b>1. Technical Crawl Architecture & Link Equity</b></summary>
  <br />
  <p><b>Issue:</b> Orphaned and low-value URLs were diluting crawl budget away from core commercial pages.</p>
  <p><b>Fix:</b> Re-architected internal link hierarchies, enforced strict canonical tags, pruned non-performing indexation, and optimized server-side rendering pipelines for CWV.</p>
  <p><b>Result:</b> Improved crawl efficiency for primary commercial landing pages and accelerated re-indexing speeds for newly published content.</p>
</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>2. High Traffic, Low Pipeline → Rebuilt BOFU On-Page & Content Strategy</b></summary>
  <br />
  <p><b>Issue:</b> Top-of-funnel content generated search volume but lacked direct lead conversion paths.</p>
  <p><b>Fix:</b> Overhauled on-page architecture across 100+ URLs. Embedded dynamic CTAs, customer testimonials, clear service tier definitions, optimized meta tags, and transparent pricing models directly above the fold.</p>
  <p><b>Result:</b> Increased conversion efficiency from organic discovery to lead capture form submission.</p>
</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>3. Backlinks & Off-Page Equity Distribution</b></summary>
  <br />
  <p><b>Issue:</b> Core revenue landing pages lagged behind competitors in page-level domain authority.</p>
  <p><b>Fix:</b> Directed off-page backlink acquisition toward high-intent commercial URLs (BOFU) using anchor text aligned with target commercial queries.</p>
  <p><b>Result:</b> Strengthened page authority and stabilized rankings for highly competitive B2B keywords during algorithm core updates.</p>
</details>

<details>
  <summary><b>4. Recommendations Stalling in Engineering → Specs Over Tickets</b></summary>
  <br />
  <p><b>Issue:</b> SEO recommendations were stalling in engineering backlogs due to lack of technical clarity and business context.</p>
  <p><b>Fix:</b> Created an ICE-based prioritization framework and drafted developer-ready specs complete with expected business ROI and precise acceptance criteria.</p>
  <p><b>Result:</b> Reduced ticket lead time and ensured technical sprint capacity was consistently allocated to high-ROI growth tasks.</p>
</details>
