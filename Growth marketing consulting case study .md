# 04 — KEY BUSINESS PROBLEMS

## 01 — Search Performance Changed, But the Real Problem Was Traffic Mix

### Situation

One account experienced a **28% decline in organic traffic** and an **8% decline in organic leads** during a period of changing search performance.

### Task

Determine whether the decline represented a broad acquisition problem or a change in **traffic quality and mix**.

### Action

I analysed:

**Traffic Mix → Search Intent → Lost Pages/Queries → High-Intent Traffic → Landing-Page CVR → Leads**

High-intent traffic remained stable, while much of the lost traffic was lower intent.

I prioritised **SEO hygiene, high-value page optimisation, content recovery and CRO** instead of recommending a blanket website rebuild or mass content production.

### Result

The response shifted from **“recover all traffic”** to **protecting and recovering commercially valuable demand**.

---

## 02 — Demand Existed, But Conversion Was the Constraint

### Situation

Where relevant demand already existed but conversion was weak, simply increasing acquisition would not necessarily solve the business problem.

### Task

Identify where the funnel was leaking.

### Action

Reviewed:

**Intent → Landing Page → Messaging → CTA → Trust → UX → Conversion**

Prioritised relevant **CRO, landing-page, messaging, CTA, trust and UX improvements**, while using paid acquisition selectively where appropriate.

### Result

The growth sequence shifted from:

**Acquire more traffic →**

to:

**Improve conversion → Validate → Scale**

## 03 — CPL Looked Efficient, But Sales Questioned Lead Quality

### Task

### Action

```text

 ↓
Opportunity
```

Analysed **intent, source, lead quality, CPQL, opportunity rate, CAC and downstream value**.

### Result

Performance evaluation moved beyond **platform efficiency** toward **commercial lead quality and sales progression**.
