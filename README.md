#  How I Think About Marketing

I don't start with:

> **"Which channel should we use?"**

I start with:

> **"What is actually stopping this business from growing?"**

My working process:

```text
Business Objective
        ↓
Market & Competitors
        ↓
Customer / Buyer
        ↓
Data & Research
        ↓
Funnel
        ↓
Bottleneck
        ↓
Hypothesis
        ↓
Strategy / Experiment
        ↓
Execution
        ↓
Measurement
        ↓
Learning
        ↓
Iteration
```

The framework isn't the differentiator.

The discipline of **not jumping from a brief straight into execution** is.

Marketing is not just about generating leads.

For me, it is about turning **market and customer understanding into qualified demand and business ROI.**

That means knowing when the answer is:

**SEO · Content · Performance · CRO · Brand · Product · Analytics · Sales alignment · Technology · Automation**

—and when it is a combination of several.

#  A Case Study: Wishup.co 

### From SEO execution to broader growth thinking

**Wishup.co · U.S. Market · Sep 2023 – Dec 2024**

Wishup was an important transition point in my career.

I joined through digital marketing/SEO, was promoted from **Digital Marketing Intern to SEO Executive within 2–3 months**, and later took on **interim team-lead responsibilities** during a leadership transition.

My work eventually connected:

**SEO · Content · Engineering · Product · Performance · Sales · Leadership**

### The problem

Organic growth wasn't one isolated SEO issue.

The work involved technical health, crawlability, indexation, website architecture, search intent, content, internal linking and coordination with technical teams.

The challenge became:

> **How do you turn SEO observations into prioritised work that other teams can actually execute?**

### What I owned

**Technical SEO**

Crawlability · Indexation · Canonicalisation · Robots.txt · Core Web Vitals · Structured Data · Site Architecture

**Content & Search**

Keyword Research · Search Intent · Content Optimisation · Metadata · Internal Linking · Content Gaps · Competitor Analysis

**Off-page**

Backlink Analysis · Outreach Support · Competitor Benchmarking

### What changed in my role

I moved from:

**Finding problems**

to:

**Finding → Prioritising → Translating → Coordinating → Validating**

I worked with engineering and product teams to translate marketing requirements into actionable implementation.

I also learned to communicate the same problem differently:

**Engineering:** what needs to change
**Content:** what the user needs
**Sales:** what customers are asking or objecting to
**Leadership:** what matters and why

That cross-functional translation became one of the most valuable skills I developed.

### Interim leadership

During a leadership transition, I coordinated priorities across **development, product, growth, sales and marketing**, while supporting work around buyer personas, keyword research, content implementation, website category launches and technical/content execution.

The lesson wasn't simply about managing tasks.

It was about **creating clarity when multiple teams have competing priorities and dependencies.**
